Corporate Partnerships

How Can Companies Partner with Fudumala?

Whether you are a small business or a large corporation, there is a partnership model that works for you. From the School Champion tier at R10,000 to custom bulk packages, we will work with your team to create a meaningful CSI initiative.

  • Logo placement on the Fudumala School Jersey Drive website and campaign materials
  • Social media features and shout-outs
  • Invitation to jersey handover events at schools
  • Photo and video content from deliveries for your CSI reporting
  • Employee engagement campaign support and materials
  • Custom sponsorship packages for larger commitments

Frequently Asked Questions About Getting Involved

What are the ways to get involved with Fudumala?

You can sponsor a jersey, spread the word about the campaign on social media, or set up a corporate partnership.

How much does it cost to sponsor a jersey?

Sponsorship starts at R350 for one jersey, with tiers available for multiple jerseys.

What if I cannot afford to sponsor right now?

You can still help by spreading the word: share the campaign on social media, tag 3 people, and use the hashtag #FudumalaUbuntuChallenge.

What is the tag-3 mechanic?

It is the mechanic that makes the Ubuntu Challenge spread: sponsor a jersey (or share if you cannot sponsor), post about it, tag 3 people, and they do the same.

How do companies partner with Fudumala?

Companies can partner through bulk sponsorships, employee engagement campaigns, and branded partnerships, including the School Champion tier at R10,000.

What do corporate partners receive?

Corporate partners receive logo placement on the website and campaign materials, social media features, invitations to handover events, photo and video content for CSI reporting, employee engagement support, and custom sponsorship packages.

What hashtag should I use when I post?

Use #FudumalaUbuntuChallenge on every post so the campaign can track the movement.

How do I contact Fudumala about a corporate partnership?

Email info@threadsforikasi.org to discuss corporate partnership options.
