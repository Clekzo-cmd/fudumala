# Fudumala School Jersey Drive 🎓

Ubuntu in Action! The Fudumala School Jersey Drive, powered by Threads for Ikasi Foundation, brings warmth, dignity, and hope to learners across South Africa.

## 🌟 About

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive is a community-driven initiative that provides school jerseys and complete uniform packs to learners in need. Since 2023, we've delivered over 570 jerseys to 12 partner schools, with a 100% learner satisfaction rate.
