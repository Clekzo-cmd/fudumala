Celebrating Women Who Lead With Purpose

Khabo Mnguni on Newzroom Afrika

← Back to News
Published: September 1, 2025

We are absolutely beaming with pride as we celebrate our incredible CEO, Khabonina Mnguni, who recently graced the screens of Newzroom Afrika this past Sunday. Khabonina's insightful interview shed light on the inspiring journey and profound mission behind both ZS Uniforms and Threads for Ikasi.

As the first runner-up in the highly competitive Access Bank and Regenesis School of Entrepreneurship pitch competition, Khabonina stood out not just for her innovative business acumen, but for her unwavering dedication to community empowerment and sustainable job creation. In her interview, she spoke passionately about the unique challenges faced by black women in the entrepreneurial landscape here in South Africa, particularly in Tembisa and across Gauteng. She powerfully articulated how ZS Uniforms and Threads for Ikasi are not just businesses, but vehicles for tangible change, fostering growth and providing vital opportunities within our communities.

Khabonina's vision to expand, innovate, and create even more meaningful employment opportunities right here in our townships is a continuous source of inspiration for our entire team and the communities we serve. Under her dynamic leadership, we are excited to continue making a significant and positive impact, transforming lives and building a brighter future.

We invite you to watch the full interview and witness Khabonina's powerful message for yourself. Her story is a testament to resilience, innovation, and the profound impact of genuine leadership.

Watch the full interview here: https://youtu.be/absR4Qr0K_M

Join us in celebrating Khabonina Mnguni and all the trailblazing women entrepreneurs who are redefining success and shaping the future of business in South Africa!
