Questions

Frequently Asked

Where does my money go?

100% of your sponsorship goes towards manufacturing and delivering school jerseys. Each jersey costs R350 to produce through our manufacturing partner, ZS Uniforms.

How do I know my jersey was delivered?

You will receive updates via email and social media when jerseys are delivered to schools. School Champion sponsors are invited to attend the handover in person.

Can I sponsor from outside South Africa?

Yes. PayFast supports international card payments. You can sponsor from anywhere in the world.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive operates under the Threads for Ikasi Foundation. Contact us at sponsor@threadsforikasi.org for tax documentation.
