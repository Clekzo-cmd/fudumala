The Steps
1

Start date

We are thrilled to announce that our sponsorship drive will be commencing on 1 April 2025 and running until 1 July 2025.

During this period, we will be calling on private and corporate sponsors to join us in making a positive impact on the lives of primary and high school learners in Tembisa.
2

Handover

The School jersey handover be between from June - August 2025.
3

Sponsorship

The sponsorship amount is R350 per jersey for both Primary School and High School learners.
Sponsor a jersey
4

Beneficiaries

Our main objective is to be able to supply learners of ten schools in and around Tembisa with school jerseys.

Our target is 1000 jerseys.
