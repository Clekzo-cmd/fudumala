Why It Matters

Why School Jerseys Matter

Winter in Tembisa is cold. For many learners, a school jersey is not a uniform item they can take for granted. It is the difference between focusing in class and shivering through a lesson.

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive exists to close that gap. Through community giving and the Ubuntu Challenge, we sponsor a school jersey for R350, and every jersey goes directly to a learner through the 15 schools in Tembisa that we work with.

Between 2023 and 2025, we reached 922 learners. In 2024, a single partnership with Sandvik put 570 jerseys across 12 schools. One jersey. One child. One winter made warmer.

Fudumala School Jersey Drive FAQs

What is the Fudumala School Jersey Drive?

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive is a community campaign that helps sponsor warm school jerseys for learners in Tembisa.

How much does one school jersey cost?

One school jersey sponsorship is R350. Each contribution helps put warmth and dignity back into a learner's school day.

Who receives the sponsored jerseys?

Sponsored jerseys are delivered to learners through participating schools across Tembisa.

How does the Ubuntu Challenge work?

A sponsor gives towards a jersey, shares the campaign, tags three people, and encourages them to pass the warmth forward.

Can companies or teams sponsor multiple jerseys?

Yes. Businesses, teams, families, and individuals can sponsor one jersey or support multiple learners through larger contribution tiers.
