Why School Jerseys Matter

Winter in Tembisa is cold. For many learners, a school jersey is not a uniform item they can take for granted. It is the difference between focusing in class and shivering through a lesson. It is the difference between feeling part of something and feeling left out.

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive exists to close that gap. Through community giving and the Ubuntu Challenge, we sponsor a school jersey for R350, and every jersey goes directly to a learner through the participating schools in Tembisa that we work with.

Since 2023, we have reached over 800 learners across 15 schools, from Ecaleni LSN to Mariting Primary. In 2024, a single partnership with Sandvik put 570 jerseys into the hands of learners across 12 schools. In 2025, we expanded to the Eastern Cape. You can read about our full impact from the 2025 season and see what is possible when a community shows up.

The Ubuntu Challenge is simple. You give, you share, you tag three people, and warmth spreads. Businesses, schools, families, and individuals have all taken part. Some sponsor one jersey. Others sponsor a classroom. To understand exactly how the challenge works, the steps are straightforward and the impact is real.

One jersey. One child. One winter made warmer.

Fudumala School Jersey Drive FAQs

What is the Fudumala School Jersey Drive?

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive is a community campaign that helps sponsor warm school jerseys for learners in Tembisa.

How much does one school jersey cost?

One school jersey sponsorship is R350. Each contribution helps put warmth and dignity back into a learner's school day.

Who receives the sponsored jerseys?

Sponsored jerseys are delivered to learners through participating schools across Tembisa.

How does the Ubuntu Challenge work?

A sponsor gives towards a jersey, shares the campaign, tags three people, and encourages them to pass the warmth forward.

Can companies or teams sponsor multiple jerseys?

Yes. Businesses, teams, families, and individuals can sponsor one jersey or support multiple learners through larger contribution tiers.
