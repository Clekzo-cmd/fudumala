How the Ubuntu Challenge Works

Four simple steps. One powerful chain of giving.

4

Steps to join

R350

Starting cost

#FudumalaUbuntuChallenge

Hashtag

15 in Tembisa

Schools reached

ZS Uniforms

Manufacturing partner

1

Sponsor a Jersey

Choose your sponsorship tier starting at R350 for one jersey. Your contribution goes directly to manufacturing a warm school jersey through ZS Uniforms, our trusted manufacturing partner. Every cent is accounted for.

2

Post About It

Once you have sponsored, share your act of warmth on social media. Use the hashtag #FudumalaUbuntuChallenge so we can track the movement and celebrate every contribution. You will receive a digital badge to share.

3

Tag 3 People

Challenge 3 friends, family members, or colleagues to take the Ubuntu Challenge. Tag them in your post and invite them to sponsor a jersey too. This is how the chain of warmth grows.

4

They Do the Same

Each person you tag sponsors a jersey, posts about it, and tags 3 more people. The cycle continues and the warmth spreads exponentially across communities, cities, and even countries.

Who Can Take the Challenge?

The Ubuntu Challenge is open to everyone. Here is how different groups can get involved.

Individuals

Anyone with R350 and a heart for community. Students, professionals, parents, grandparents, everyone is welcome.

Companies

Businesses looking to make a tangible social impact. Bulk sponsorships, employee engagement campaigns, and branded partnership opportunities.

Groups and Communities

Churches, sports clubs, stokvel groups, school boards, and any community organisation that wants to rally together for a cause.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Ubuntu Challenge

How much does it cost to start the Ubuntu Challenge?

R350 sponsors one jersey and is the starting point for taking the Ubuntu Challenge.

What do I do after I sponsor a jersey?

Share your act of warmth on social media using the hashtag #FudumalaUbuntuChallenge, so it can be tracked and celebrated. You will receive a digital badge to share.

What happens when I tag 3 people?

Each person you tag can sponsor a jersey, post about it, and tag 3 more people. The cycle continues and the warmth spreads across communities.

Who manufactures the Fudumala jerseys?

ZS Uniforms is the trusted manufacturing partner, producing every jersey sponsored through the Ubuntu Challenge.

Can companies take the Ubuntu Challenge?

Yes. Companies can take part through bulk sponsorships, employee engagement campaigns, and branded partnership opportunities.

Can groups and community organisations join?

Yes. Churches, sports clubs, stokvel groups, school boards, and other community organisations are welcome to take the challenge together.

Where do the jerseys go?

Every jersey sponsored is delivered to one of the 15 schools Fudumala currently serves across Tembisa.

Do I get anything for sponsoring a jersey?

Yes, you receive a digital badge to share on social media as proof of your sponsorship.
