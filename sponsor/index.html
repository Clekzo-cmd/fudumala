Why It Matters

A Jersey Is More Than Warmth

Winter in Tembisa arrives without warning. For many learners across the township's schools, a school jersey is the only layer standing between them and the cold. Without it, attendance drops, concentration fades, and learning suffers. A single jersey, costing just R350, changes that completely.

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive was built on a straightforward idea rooted in the Ubuntu Challenge: one person sponsors, tags three people, and each of those people does the same. A single act of warmth multiplies across a community. Between 2023 and 2025, this model reached 922 learners across Tembisa, and in 2025 the campaign reached the Eastern Cape for the first time.

In 2026 the goal is 1,000 jerseys, spread across 15 schools in Tembisa. Every jersey is manufactured locally by ZS Uniforms and delivered directly to learners through their schools. Nothing gets lost between the sponsor and the child. You can see exactly where your contribution goes.

The impact is real and documented. In the 2025 season, 262 learners were reached. School staff reported improved attendance on cold mornings. Parents shared photos of children wearing their jerseys on the first day they arrived. Sponsors who chose the School Champion tier attended handover events at schools and saw the reaction firsthand.

Whether you sponsor one jersey at R350 or champion a school at R10,000, your contribution is tracked in real time on the jersey counter at the top of this page. Every payment triggers an update. You can watch the number grow. When we hit 1,000, every learner across all 15 schools will have a jersey in their hands before the coldest month of the year.

If you want to understand the full chain from sponsor to learner, read how the campaign works. If you are ready to act, choose a tier above and complete your sponsorship in under two minutes through PayFast. One jersey. One learner. One winter made warmer.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Where does my money go?

100% of your sponsorship goes towards manufacturing and delivering school jerseys. Each jersey costs R350 to produce through our manufacturing partner, ZS Uniforms.

How do I know my jersey was delivered?

You will receive updates via email and social media when jerseys are delivered to schools. School Champion sponsors are invited to attend the handover in person.

Can I sponsor from outside South Africa?

Yes. PayFast supports international card payments. You can sponsor from anywhere in the world.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive operates under the Threads for Ikasi Foundation. Contact us at info@threadsforikasi.org for tax documentation.
