About the Fudumala School Jersey Drive

Warmth, dignity, and community, one jersey at a time.

Our Purpose

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive exists to provide school jerseys to learners in need and restore their sense of dignity. A warm jersey is more than clothing. It is a signal that someone cares, that a child belongs, and that learning can happen without the distraction of cold.

Threads for Ikasi Foundation

The community organisation that powers the Fudumala School Jersey Drive. Dedicated to uplifting township communities through clothing, education, and sustainable development.

ZS Uniforms

Our trusted manufacturing partner. ZS Uniforms produces every Fudumala jersey with care, ensuring quality, durability, and proper school branding for each school we serve.

Our Journey

From a community idea to a movement reaching 1,000 jerseys.

2023

Early Beginnings

The Fudumala School Jersey Drive started as a community initiative to address the need for school jerseys among learners in Tembisa. The first jerseys were distributed through grassroots efforts and local partnerships.

2024

Breakthrough Year

With the Sandvik corporate partnership, the Fudumala School Jersey Drive delivered 570 jerseys across 12 schools in Tembisa. This was the campaign's biggest year and proved the model could scale with the right support.

2025

Growing Our Reach

In 2025, we impacted the lives of 262 learners. We also extended our reach to the Eastern Cape, where we donated 109 Fudumala packs in partnership with Standard Bank Transaction Banking. This year proved that the warmth can travel beyond Tembisa.

2026

The Ubuntu Challenge

The launch of the Ubuntu Challenge, a viral tag-3 sponsorship model targeting 1,000 jerseys. Built on everything we have learned, with a focus on community participation and transparent impact tracking.
