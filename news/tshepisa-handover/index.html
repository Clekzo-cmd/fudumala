50 Full School Uniforms Handed Over at Tshepisa Primary School in Honour of the Late Mr Martin Kekana

Friday, 6 March 2026 · Tshepisa Primary School, Tembisa

A learner carries a full school uniform received at Tshepisa Primary School, Tembisa, 6 March 2026.

The third leg of the 1000 Uniforms Initiative was handed over in a former principal's name.

On Friday 6 March 2026, Threads for iKasi Foundation, in partnership with the Transnet Foundation, handed over 50 full school uniforms to learners at Tshepisa Primary School in Tembisa, Gauteng. The handover was held in honour of the late Mr Martin Kekana, a former principal of the school.

It was the third leg of the 1000 Uniforms Initiative, following Eersterust High School in Eersterust on 12 February 2026 and the handover at Inxiweni Primary School in Tembisa a week earlier, on 27 February 2026.

The handover honoured the late Mr Martin Kekana, a former principal of the school

Threads for iKasi Foundation dedicated the day to Mr Martin Kekana, a former principal of Tshepisa Primary School, describing his leadership as having helped shape the school community. Announcing the handover the day before, the foundation put it plainly: the way to honour him was to continue the work he had given his life to.

Each uniform was packed and labelled for a named learner before the handover began, Tshepisa Primary School, 6 March 2026. (Learners' names on the labels have been obscured.)

The Transnet Foundation funded all 50 full school uniforms

The Transnet Foundation sponsored the handover in full. Confirming the visit afterwards, Transnet described the donation as part of the Threads for iKasi 1000 Uniforms Campaign, a programme aimed at restoring dignity and building confidence among learners in communities affected by socio-economic hardship.

Threads for iKasi Foundation thanked the Transnet Foundation for walking the journey with it and for investing in the dignity and the future of learners in Tembisa.

Learners wait in line with the bags holding their new full school uniforms, Tshepisa Primary School, 6 March 2026. (Learners' names on the labels have been obscured.)

Tshepisa was the third school in the 1000 Uniforms Initiative

The 1000 Uniforms Initiative, also called 1000 Uniforms for Tembisa's Future, sets out to place full school uniforms with learners who need them. Eersterust High School received the first 50 uniforms on 12 February 2026, in partnership with Roots 182 NPO. Inxiweni Primary School received the next 50 on 27 February 2026. Tshepisa Primary School received a further 50 on 6 March 2026.

The Inxiweni and Tshepisa handovers were both sponsored by the Transnet Foundation, and both were dedicated to educators who had served the schools receiving them.

A full school uniform removes one reason to stay home

A learner without a proper uniform is visible in a way no child wants to be. Threads for iKasi Foundation runs the 1000 Uniforms Initiative on a simple principle: a full uniform lets a learner walk in dressed like everyone else, and that is what makes it possible to arrive, take part, and stay.

The campaign remains open to new sponsors

More schools in Tembisa are waiting. Individual and corporate sponsorships are open, and a company can fund a full school handover as the Transnet Foundation did at Tshepisa Primary School.

Questions people ask about the Tshepisa handover

How many school uniforms were handed over at Tshepisa Primary School?

50 full school uniforms were handed over to learners at Tshepisa Primary School in Tembisa on Friday 6 March 2026.

When was the Tshepisa Primary School handover?

The handover took place on Friday 6 March 2026 at Tshepisa Primary School in Tembisa, Gauteng.

Who was the Tshepisa Primary School handover in honour of?

The handover was held in honour of the late Mr Martin Kekana, a former principal of Tshepisa Primary School whose leadership helped shape the school community.

Who sponsored the uniforms handed over at Tshepisa Primary School?

The Transnet Foundation sponsored the 50 full school uniforms, in partnership with Threads for iKasi Foundation.

What is the 1000 Uniforms Initiative?

The 1000 Uniforms Initiative, also called 1000 Uniforms for Tembisa's Future, is a Threads for iKasi Foundation campaign to place full school uniforms with learners who need them, so that no child is kept out of the classroom for lack of proper school attire.

Was Tshepisa the first school in the 1000 Uniforms Initiative?

No. Tshepisa Primary School was the third leg of the initiative. The first handover took place at Eersterust High School in Eersterust on 12 February 2026, in partnership with Roots 182 NPO, and the second at Inxiweni Primary School in Tembisa on 27 February 2026. Each of the three handovers placed 50 full school uniforms.

Where is Tshepisa Primary School?

Tshepisa Primary School is in Tembisa, Gauteng, South Africa.

What did each learner receive at the Tshepisa handover?

Each of the 50 learners received a full school uniform, packed into an individually labelled bag prepared for them ahead of the handover.

Who is Threads for iKasi Foundation?

Threads for iKasi Foundation is a registered South African non-profit organisation, NPO registration number 2024/352240/08, which runs the Fudumala Ubuntu Challenge and the 1000 Uniforms Initiative.

How can I sponsor a school uniform or jersey?

Sponsorships are made online at fudumala.threadsforikasi.org/sponsor. Both individual and corporate sponsorships are accepted, and the campaign is open on an ongoing basis.

Can a company sponsor a full school handover?

Yes. The Transnet Foundation funded the full 50-uniform handover at Tshepisa Primary School. Companies that want to fund a school handover can contact Threads for iKasi Foundation to arrange one.

Why do school uniforms matter for attendance?

A full school uniform lets a learner arrive dressed the same as everyone else. Threads for iKasi Foundation runs the initiative to remove the uniform as a barrier to attending and taking part in school.
